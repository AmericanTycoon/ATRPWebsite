About the Server

Welcome to American Tycoon Roleplay — a premium RedM roleplay server set in the golden age of the American frontier. We offer a rich, immersive experience where every player carves their own path through the Wild West.

Whether you're a hardworking rancher, a slick-tongued politician, a feared outlaw, or a rising oil magnate, American Tycoon RP gives you the tools, the community, and the world to make your story unforgettable.

Our server runs on a carefully crafted economy, custom scripts, and a dedicated staff team — all designed to put immersive storytelling first.

Server Features

Dynamic Economy

A player-driven economy with supply & demand. Mine gold, sell cattle, trade goods, or dominate the oil market.

Gangs & Factions

Join or create your own gang, law enforcement faction, or political group. Rise through the ranks and control territory.

Property Ownership

Purchase homesteads, ranches, saloons, and businesses. Build an empire from the ground up.

Legal System

Server Rules

Breaking these rules will result in warnings, kicks, or permanent bans.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff with respect. Racism, harassment, and hate speech of any kind are strictly prohibited.

02

Roleplay First

Stay in character at all times while in-game. Use OOC chat sparingly and appropriately. Your character's story matters.

03

No RDM / VDM

Random Deathmatch and Vehicle Deathmatch are not allowed. Every conflict must have valid in-character justification.

04

No Metagaming

Do not use out-of-character information to gain in-character advantages. What happens in Discord, stays out of game.

05

No Cheating or Exploiting

Any use of hacks, mods, or server exploits will result in an immediate and permanent ban — no exceptions.

06

Value Your Life

Act as your character would realistically act. Don't engage in reckless behavior that your character would fear.

Full rules are available in our Discord server.

How to Join

1

Own Red Dead Redemption 2

You'll need a copy of Red Dead Redemption 2 on PC (Steam, Epic Games, or Rockstar Launcher).

2

Download & Install RedM

Download the free RedM client from redm.gg and install it. It runs alongside your existing RDR2 installation.

3

Join Our Discord

Head to our Discord server, read the rules, and grab the whitelist role to gain server access.

4

Connect & Play

Launch RedM, search for "American Tycoon RP" in the server browser, and connect. Your legend begins now.
